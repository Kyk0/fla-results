## D7 (2026-11-14 02:18 EET) — metric definitions fixed BEFORE the full dataset exists (pre-registration)

Written before any full-scale result, so the gate cannot be bent afterwards.

- Model selection (epochs, hyperparameters) on **val** only. Test is evaluated once per trained model. Three seeds per input set, all reported.
- Main input sets: `P`, `P+N`, `P+N+S` (zones 2 x 4 per foot). Secondary, diagnostic only: `P+N_lp` (Fz low-passed, to test whether slip is read from the solver's normal-force chatter), `P+Ntot` and `P+Ntot+Stot` (per-foot totals instead of zones, to test whether the zone distribution matters; zone distribution in a rigid-body solver is an approximation).
- Headline numbers come from **mode = walk**, test split (test_in and test_out bands pooled; also listed separately). Stand, rig and lifted are reported per mode.
- G1 criterion 1 ("+5 points macro-F1 on hard-grip versus hard-slip"): from the 6-class model, on test windows whose true class is hard_grip or hard_slip, mode walk, flat episodes (no strips); F1 of each of the two classes with predictions as they are (a prediction of any third class counts as a miss), averaged; mean over 3 seeds; `P+N+S` minus `P+N` >= 5.0 points. A dedicated 2-class model is reported as a secondary number, not for the gate.
- G1 criterion 2 ("surface change detected at least one step earlier"): on strip episodes of the test split. Change time t0 = first touchdown of either foot on the new surface. Detection = first window end time t >= t0 at which the predicted class equals the new class for 2 consecutive windows (0.4 s). Latency in seconds and in steps (touchdowns on the new surface in [t0, t]). Episodes never detected within 5 s count as 5 s / the steps in 5 s and are also reported as a miss rate. Criterion: median latency in steps of `P+N+S` at least 1.0 lower than `P+N` (mean of per-seed medians), pooled over the strip types; per-type numbers listed.
- G1 criterion 3 ("friction MAE at least 20 % lower"): MAE of predicted mu on test windows, mode walk, at least one foot in contact, all terrain classes pooled; mean over seeds; `P+N+S` MAE <= 0.8 x `P+N` MAE. Listed separately for hard_grip + hard_slip only.
- Support state: macro-F1 over (airborne, partial, full) per foot, mean of L and R. Detection delay for edge: time from the first frame whose ground-truth label is partial at a touchdown to the first window predicting partial for that foot. Detection delay for lifted (lifted-mid episodes): time from both feet airborne (ground truth) to the first window predicting airborne for both feet, held for 2 windows.
- Window label = label at the window's last frame; windows whose terrain label is -1 are excluded from the terrain loss and terrain metrics (still used for support state).

## D14 (2026-11-14 08:59 EET) — metric definitions for v2, fixed before dataset v2 exists

General
- Dataset v2, test split (bands disjoint from train and val, as v1), mode walk unless stated. Windows 1.0 s, stride 0.2 s, label at the last frame. Three seeds per model; mean over seeds; every seed is listed.
- `P` = joint positions, joint velocities, gyro, projected gravity. No command channels (v1 showed they can flip a result); one diagnostic run of `P` with commands is kept for the v1 comparison.
- Sensor combinations: C1 = `N_real`+`S_real`, C2 = `N_real`+`S_blk`, C3 = `N_real`, C4 = `N_blk`+`S_blk`, C5 = `N_blk`. Each trained with `P` ("P+Ck") and without ("Ck"). Also `P`, `S_blk` alone, `S_real` alone.
- "Real difference" between two conditions on one metric: the difference of the seed means is larger than twice the larger of the two standard deviations over seeds (population std, as printed in the tables), and it is at least 2 points for F1 and accuracy, or at least 10 % of the reference condition's value for errors, delays and miss rates. Anything else is reported as "no difference". The reference condition is the second one named in each comparison below.
- Metrics. M1 terrain macro-F1 over the six classes. M2 support-state macro-F1 (airborne, partial, full; mean of the two feet), with the partial-class F1 listed. M3 friction MAE on windows with at least one foot in contact and a terrain label. M4 strip latency after a surface change, median seconds and steps, and miss rate. M5 edge events, as D7 (a stance that starts on partial support; detected if the foot is predicted partial at a window end inside that stance), now split by how long the partial support lasts from its first frame to the end of the stance: shorter than 0.3 s, and 0.3 s or longer; miss rate and median delay for each group. M6 lifted delay. G1-1 grip-versus-slip F1 as in D7.
- `hard-mid` (friction 0.36-0.7) is a test-only set: it is not in train or val, has no terrain label and is excluded from M1. Reported for it: friction MAE and which class each model assigns.
- Per-foot ground labels (flat, on a bump, partial support, void) are for analysis: terrain recall on rough and edge is split by whether a foot touched a bump or an edge inside the window. They do not enter the seven answers.

1. Realistic pressure map against four blocks: P+C3 versus P+C5, and C3 versus C5. Metrics M1, M2 (macro and partial), M3, M5 (long events, miss rate). 3. Per-point shear over block shear: P+C1 versus P+C2, and C1 versus C2. Same metrics as question 2. Reported with the label of D13: the simulator gives per-point shear no independent content.
4. Transfer to an unseen gait: leave-one-gait-out over the walking sources available (four if the new policy is accepted, otherwise three, stated).
